Our Story

Bridging the gap between potential and opportunity since 2015.

The Challenge

The Digital Divide in Africa

While the world moves toward a fully digital economy, millions of young Africans are being left behind. In rural communities, access to a computer is a luxury, and stable internet is a dream. This is not just a lack of gadgets — it is a lack of opportunity.

EduBridge Africa was founded to ensure that a child's postal code does not determine their digital future. We believe every young person deserves the tools and mentorship to thrive in the modern economy.
