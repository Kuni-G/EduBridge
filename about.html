Our Story

Bridging the gap between potential and opportunity since 2015.

The Challenge

The Digital Divide in Africa

While the world moves toward a fully digital economy, millions of young Africans are being left behind. In rural communities, access to a computer is a luxury, and stable internet is a dream. This isn't just a lack of gadgets—it's a lack of opportunity.

EduBridge Africa was founded to ensure that a child's postal code doesn't determine their digital future.
