Our Impact Programs

From infrastructure to mentorship, we provide a holistic approach to bridging the digital divide in Africa.

Education

Digital Skills for Girls

We are dedicated to closing the gender gap in the tech industry. Our specialized bootcamps provide young women with high-demand skills in coding, UI/UX design, and digital marketing, paired with leadership training to help them step into the tech workforce with confidence.

Infrastructure

Rural Tech Hubs

Geography should not limit potential. We equip rural schools with solar-powered computer labs and satellite internet, creating a gateway for students to access global learning resources and online courses. Each hub serves as a community anchor for digital learning.

Mentorship

Mentorship Match

Connecting eager students with tech professionals from around the world. Through weekly one-on-one calls, students receive career guidance, portfolio reviews, and the confidence to pursue global opportunities in the technology industry.
