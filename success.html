Before and After EduBridge

The tangible difference our programs make in communities.

Before

Only 12% of students in partner villages had ever used a computer. Internet access was virtually non-existent, and digital career paths seemed impossible for rural youth.

After

Over 85% of students now have regular computer access. 40% of graduates have gone on to earn income through tech-related work or further education in STEM fields.
